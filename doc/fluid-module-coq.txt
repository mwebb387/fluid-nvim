INTRO                                                  *fluid-module-coq-intro*

This module is designed to integrate ms-jpq/coq_nvim, a fast completion
engine for Neovim with extensive language support and smart completion
features. Currently under development and not yet implemented.

Basic usage: >

Note: This module is currently incomplete and not functional.
